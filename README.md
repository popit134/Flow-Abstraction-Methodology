# Flow Abstraction
*C-Flow is an Alternative Word that means Core Flow*

A [flow state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_(psychology)) game design methodology for retention and lucidity through inherent flow.

It prioritizes baseline dynamics or pure sensation as the gravity of the game concept.

*"Dynamic" in this context means the motion or nature of the sensation and communication of the game broader than behaviors and actions.*

**The Flow Abstraction Theory states it is easier to set inherent flow dynamics first than prioritizing the other elements that alter it.**

Involves psychological flow abstraction to analyze the fundamental sensation through baseline dynamics.

First determine your target audience. Then define a dynamic they will do that won't break their flow.

"The Tactical Crisp of Loot Against Zombie Targets"
- Evaluates the fundamental splendor if it passes taste test & custom criteria.
- Captures the game essence hence allowing relevant expansion.

## Constructive Depth
A characteristic of balance, optimism, and depth thinking useful for game analysis & update.
- Analyzing thoroughly with genuine judgement and transparency.
> Example: Enchancing a weak critical point that players desire.

- Focusing on depth rather than the sole quantity
> Example: Deepening the mechanics through subtraction of use and addition of suspense in gameplay.

> Against: Adding excessive mechanics & elements that result into bad guesswork of the objective.

## River Effect
Structured flows achieve ultimate retention or hook.
> A great dynamics communicate the players about the concepts and intended message utilized for intellectual flow—a succesful conscious negotiation between players for favor. 
> Example: Daily Rewards & Leaderboard Aspiration

Game modes and multitudes of game divergence are valid for C-Flow.


# Intended Benefits of C-Flow

- **Organic Player Retention:** Anchors engagement entirely in pure, baseline sensation and identity.

- **Leaner Codebases & Pipelines:** Having Constructive Depth lessens the developer' temptation of fluff. 

- **Frictionless Scalability:** Establishes the baseline dynamic as the main gravity. Hence it allows UI, narrative scripts, and extra modes to scale without diluting the core experience.

- **Resource Efficiency:** Locking this gravity early eliminates late-stage loop adjustments, reducing production budget and development time by **up to 40%**.

# Tutorial Steps

3. Analyze then expand, notice details not by rigid checkmarks alone but how players will feel, and think to achieve your vision about the game.
